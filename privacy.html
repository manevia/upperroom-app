Upper Room Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026 · Upper Room is made by Yeshua Impact

Upper Room helps you capture sermons, study the Bible and pray with others. We collect as little as we can, never sell your data, and never use it for advertising.

What stays on your iPhone

Sermon recordings, transcripts, Sermon Maps, notes, highlights, journal entries, memory verses and study plans are stored only on your iPhone. Speech recognition runs on your device when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple processes the audio under Apple’s privacy policy. Sermon summaries use Apple Intelligence on your device.

What we keep when you create an account

  • Account: your email address, name, and (if you add them) your church, pastor and profile photo.
  • The Room: prayer requests you share (with the city you type, rounded so it never shows an exact spot), posts, Amens, the people you follow and block, the prayers you mark, the rooms you host, and reports you send.
  • Live rooms: your voice is streamed live to the people in the room through LiveKit. We don’t record live rooms.

Anonymous prayer requests never show your name or photo to others.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase: account and community data storage.
  • LiveKit: live audio for rooms.
  • Crossway (ESV API), helloao.org and ElevenLabs: Bible text, commentary and narration. These receive only the Bible passage being requested, not your personal information.

Safety and moderation

To keep the community safe, our team may review reported content and the account that shared it, hide content that breaks our community promise, and ban accounts.

Your choices

  • Delete any post or prayer request you shared from its ••• menu.
  • Delete your account any time in the app: Profile → Delete Account. This removes your account, profile, photo and community content from our servers.
  • Use Upper Room without an account; only the Room needs one.

Children

Upper Room is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their data.
